My Story

"My peak rank in Valorant is **Immortal 3 (400 RR)**. I know how a crisp sensor should feel. When the mouse starts to feel like 'mud,' it's a technical issue, not a bad day."

The Upgrade Trap: MSI A750GL vs. Old Wiring

I upgraded to a high-end **MSI MAG A750GL (ATX 3.0)** PSU. Ironically, things got worse. Because my 60-year-old apartment lacks grounding, this high-performance PSU had nowhere to dump the electrical noise. My microphone was literally shocking me, and the mouse felt delayed.

Mouse Testing

RAWM ER21PRO: Unplayable. Sensor spun out constantly.
Razer DeathAdder V2: The only one that remained "okay," likely due to better internal shielding, but even it eventually felt heavy under 180V load.

Currently replacing wiring with Hager components. Updates coming soon.
