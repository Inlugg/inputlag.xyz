Input Lag Lab

Hi. This project is dedicated to researching and eliminating the impact of the electrical component on input lag in competitive gaming.

What is Input Lag, really?

Input Lag is the time it takes for your system to turn a physical action into a visible result on the screen. It’s often confused with ping, but they are different. Ping is network latency; Input Lag is internal latency.

Why are we hunting this?

In games like Valorant or CS, every millisecond counts. High input lag makes your aim feel "floaty" or "heavy," as if your crosshair is attached to a rubber band.

Note: We look beyond Windows tweaks, focusing on how power grid quality and grounding issues affect your performance.
