Electricity and Input Lag

1. Power Grids: Then and Now

Modern devices use Switching Power Supplies (SMPS). They dump high-frequency "noise" back into your outlets. Without proper grounding, this electrical noise leaks into your PC case and USB ports.

2. High Polling Rate Paradox

Modern mice running at 1000Hz - 8000Hz are extremely sensitive to micro-fluctuations in the power grid that weren't noticeable 15 years ago.

3. Data Distortion

When the grid is "dirty," the mouse controller spends vital microseconds on error correction. You feel this as a loss of responsiveness.

4. Why No Grounding Kills Your "Reg"

Without grounding, your PC case can have a 110V potential. For a microchip, this is like trying to use super-accurate scales while someone is constantly shaking the table.
